# Fake Payment Screenshot & TID Verifier

A high-speed fraud prevention application designed for Pakistani shopkeepers, fuel station operators, and online merchants to detect forged payment screenshots in 2 seconds, verify Transaction IDs (TID) against real merchant SMS inboxes (3737, 8558, banks, Raast), and trigger audio sirens upon forgery detection.

## User Review & Critical Decisions

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The following user preferences were confirmed in Phase 1 and will govern implementation:

- **Confirmed Decision 1 (Language & Audio Alert Style)**: English interface paired with high-urgency audio siren tones and spoken voice alerts ("Payment Verified: Rupees X confirmed" vs "Warning: Fake payment detected! TID not found in SMS inbox").
- **Confirmed Decision 2 (TID Verification Source)**: Real SMS inbox reader only (`Telephony.Sms` query with `READ_SMS` runtime permission) targeted at official merchant sender codes (3737 for EasyPaisa, 8558 for JazzCash, SadaPay, NayaPay, Raast, and major Pakistani banks like Meezan, HBL, UBL, Bank Alfalah).
- **Confirmed Decision 3 (Supported Payment Networks)**: Specialized parsing and forensic detection tailored for EasyPaisa, JazzCash, NayaPay, SadaPay, and Raast / Inter-Bank Transfer screenshots.

## 1. Overview & Core Concept

### What It Does
When a customer shows or shares a payment confirmation screenshot, the shopkeeper imports or photographs it. Within 2 seconds, the app performs a two-tier forensic check:
1. **Error Level Analysis (ELA) & Visual Forensics**: Computes JPEG compression level discrepancies and font-edge anomalies to reveal digitally edited text (pasted amounts, swapped recipient names, altered dates, or counterfeit templates generated by fake apps).
2. **Instant TID & Amount Cross-Matching**: Extracts the Transaction ID (TID), exact Amount, Date, and Provider via OCR / Multimodal AI, then scans the shopkeeper's real SMS inbox for official incoming transaction messages received around that timestamp.
3. **Multi-Sensory Verdict (Siren / Safe)**:
   - **Genuine (Match Found)**: Crisp green checkmark, haptic tap, and calm voice announcement ("Payment of Rs. [Amount] verified").
   - **Fraudulent / Unverified (No SMS Match or High ELA Anomaly)**: Bright flashing red siren banner, loud pulsating emergency siren tone (`ToneGenerator` / `MediaPlayer`), and urgent spoken voice alert ("Warning! Fake payment detected. No matching bank SMS found").

### Target Audience
Retail shopkeepers, cashiers, fuel station staff, mobile shop owners, and delivery merchants who face quick "customer-standing-at-counter" pressure.

## 2. User Experience & Visual Design

### Visual Identity & Theme
- **Theme Philosophy**: Utilitarian, mission-critical, high-contrast security terminal.
- **Palette**:
  - Dark Charcoal base (`#0E1116`) and deep surface (`#161B22`) to maximize visibility under sunlight and keep counter glare low.
  - Urgent Alert Crimson (`#FF3B30`) for counterfeit/unmatched flags.
  - Verified Emerald (`#34C759`) for verified transactions.
  - Forensic Cyber Cyan (`#00D2FF`) for ELA heatmap overlays and scan grids.
  - High-readability pure white (`#FFFFFF`) and slate (`#8B949E`) typography.
- **Typography**: Clean, bold sans-serif with prominent display figures for Rupee amounts and monospace styling for Transaction IDs (TID).

### Key User Flows
1. **Counter Quick Scan (Home Screen)**:
   - Primary action: "Scan Payment Screenshot" (Photo Picker or Camera capture).
   - Instant 2-second processing with animated radar scanner indicator.
   - Result Card displaying:
     - Prominent status badge: `MATCHED & VERIFIED`, `ALERT: TID NOT IN SMS`, or `TAMPERED TEXT DETECTED`.
     - Extracted Data: Provider (EasyPaisa/JazzCash/SadaPay/Raast), Amount (e.g., Rs. 4,500), TID (e.g., 28491823910), Timestamp.
     - SMS Match Status: Matched official SMS snippet with matching sender (e.g. `From: 3737 - EasyPaisa`) and time delta (e.g. `Received 1 min ago`).
     - ELA Forensic Heatmap toggle: Tap to view highlighted pixel error regions showing where numbers or text were digitally edited.
2. **Foreground Audio & Siren**:
   - Immediate audio feedback upon scan completion: distinct safe chime vs pulsing high-pitch emergency alarm siren.
   - Voice alert synthesis via Android Text-To-Speech (TTS) for hands-free confirmation in busy shop environments.
   - Mute / Stop Siren button clearly accessible.
3. **SMS Inbox Sync & Diagnostic Tab**:
   - Permission status banner with clear shopkeeper explanation ("SMS permission is required to verify real incoming payment receipts from 3737, 8558, and banks").
   - Live list of recently received merchant payment SMS messages for manual lookup and sanity check.
4. **Audit History & Scam Log**:
   - Local Room database log of all verified and flagged scans with thumbnail, verdict, risk score, and date.
   - Filter by `All`, `Fraudulent`, or `Verified`.

## 3. Key Product Decisions & Trade-Offs

- **Client-Side ELA Scan + Multimodal Anomaly Inspection**:
  - *Chosen Approach*: On-device Error Level Analysis algorithm (`Bitmap` recompression delta at 90% quality to compute error map) combined with Gemini Multimodal Vision analysis (`gemini-3.8-flash`) to detect font irregularities, mismatched canvas dimensions, fake generator artifacts, and extract structured TID/Amount/Sender data.
  - *Why*: Gives instant visual proof (ELA heatmap) for the shopkeeper while providing deep cognitive validation against fake app templates.
- **Real SMS Reader with Targeted Telephony Query**:
  - *Chosen Approach*: Query `content://sms/inbox` filtering by merchant addresses (3737, 8558, SadaPay, NayaPay, Raast, HBL, UBL, Meezan, Alfalah) and regex matching for TID patterns.
  - *Why*: Fulfills the user's explicit preference ("Real SMS inbox reader only") while ensuring zero false matches from random contacts.
- **Local Persistence via Room**:
  - *Chosen Approach*: Store scan logs, ELA score, extracted TID, amount, verdict, and timestamp in local Room DB.
  - *Why*: Fast, offline, private, and does not require shopkeepers to sign up for accounts to retain dispute records.

### Data Models & State Machine
- `VerificationResult`:
  - `id: Long`
  - `provider: PaymentProvider` (EasyPaisa, JazzCash, NayaPay, SadaPay, Raast, OtherBank)
  - `extractedTid: String`
  - `extractedAmount: Double`
  - `extractedDate: String`
  - `elaAnomalyScore: Float` (0.0 to 1.0)
  - `smsMatched: Boolean`
  - `smsSender: String?`
  - `smsBody: String?`
  - `verdict: FraudVerdict` (`VERIFIED_GENUINE`, `COUNTERFEIT_NO_SMS`, `SUSPICIOUS_TAMPERED_IMAGE`, `UNRESOLVED`)
  - `timestamp: Long`
- `VerificationUiState`:
  - `Idle`
  - `Scanning(stage: String)` (e.g., "Computing ELA Heatmap...", "Reading SMS Inbox...")
  - `Result(result: VerificationResult, heatmapBitmap: Bitmap?, isSirenPlaying: Boolean)`
  - `Error(message: String)`

### Audio & Siren Engine
- `ToneGenerator(AudioManager.STREAM_ALARM, 100)` configured with high-intensity alarm tones (`TONE_CDMA_EMERGENCY_RINGBACK` / `TONE_CDMA_ALERT_AUTOREDIAL`).
- `TextToSpeech` engine configured with English voice for clear audible announcements.
